## P1: Critical Research-Creation Profile · Pairs

# Version 5: Final Profile

**Goal:** Revise and publish the final Critical Research-Creation Profile on the course Webador website. Submit a ZIP package containing a PDF record of the profile and all image files used on the webpage.

> This is the public version of the project. Review all writing, citations, links, images, credits, and alt text before publishing.

## What you will complete

- A completed artist or collective profile on Webador
- A PDF containing the complete profile information
- An `Images` folder containing every image uploaded to the webpage
- One ZIP package containing the PDF and image folder

## Division of labour

Both partners are responsible for the accuracy and completion of the webpage and ZIP package. Divide the initial work fairly, but review every section together before publishing.

| Partner A | Partner B |
|---|---|
| Revise assigned written sections | Revise assigned written sections |
| Prepare and check assigned images | Prepare and check assigned images |
| Add assigned content to Webador | Add assigned content to Webador |
| Check assigned citations, links, captions, and alt text | Check assigned citations, links, captions, and alt text |

Both partners must review the complete webpage on a computer and mobile device. The final page should use a consistent voice, visual structure, and citation style.

> Do not divide the profile into two isolated halves. Each partner is responsible for understanding and checking the complete project.

## 2. Prepare the final content

Revise the writing and visual materials from Versions 1–3 and the poster session before uploading them. Use your own words and keep the writing clear and specific. Correct incomplete citations, broken links, missing captions, low-quality images, and unclear alt text.

The webpage must contain the following sections in this order.

### 1. Artist or collective

Include:

- Artist or collective name as the main page title
- One high-quality vertical profile image
- Revised **100–150-word artist or collective profile**
  - Relevant location and cultural, social, political, or historical context
  - Media, materials, technologies, methods, and themes connected to the research

The profile image must include:

- Caption
- Photographer or creator credit
- Alt text

### 2. Selected artworks or projects

Complete each subsection with the two selected artworks or projects.

For each work, include:

- Title and year
- Medium, materials, technologies, duration, dimensions, or format
- **50–100-word description** of what the audience sees, hears, encounters, or does
- One to three images or other forms of documentation
- Stable link to the artwork or project when available

Every image or item of documentation must include:

- Caption
- Image or media credit
- Alt text

> For time-based, interactive, or performance work, embed or link to reliable documentation when possible. Do not upload video or audio files without permission to publish them.

### 3. Research focus

Include:

- Main research question
- Two supporting research questions
- Revised thesis statement responding to the research questions
- Two central concepts guiding the analysis

> Present this information concisely so readers can understand the direction of the profile before reading the full analysis.

### 4. Critical analysis

Include:

- Full names of both student authors
- Revised **500–700-word critical analysis**
- APA in-text citations

The analysis must retain the structure developed in Version 3:

- Introduction establishing the research question, thesis, concepts, and artworks
- First critical connection developed through Artwork or Project 1
- Second critical connection developed through Artwork or Project 2
- Conclusion bringing the argument together and identifying an important tension, limit, or unresolved question

> Use specific evidence from the artworks and bring the course readings and other sources into conversation. Do not present separate summaries of each source.

### 5. Annotated bibliography

Include the revised annotated bibliography for all eight core sources:

| Source type | Required |
|---|---:|
| Course readings | 2 |
| Additional academic sources | 2 |
| Primary artist sources | 2 |
| Critical or contextual sources | 2 |

Each entry must include:

- Complete APA reference
- Working link or DOI when available
- Revised annotation

> Use consistent APA formatting and organize the entries alphabetically by the first author's last name.

### 6. Research-creation proposal

Include:

- Representative Image (with caption and alt text)
- Title of the proposed work
- Revised **100–150-word concept statement**
  - Explanation of how the proposal responds to, extends, questions, or translates part of the artist's practice
  - Proposed medium, method, technology, audience, or context
  - Relevant ethical considerations

### 7. Research poster

Upload the final poster as a high-quality `.JPG` or `.PNG` image.

Include:

- A brief caption
- Alt text describing the poster's main content, structure, images, and visual approach

> Use the final poster version presented during the poster session, including any necessary corrections made afterward. The poster must be readable when opened or enlarged on the webpage.
