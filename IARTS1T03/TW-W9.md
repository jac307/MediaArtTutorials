<h1 style="color: darkred;">W9: Technical Walkthrough Activity — Re-Designing Sound and Light for the LFS Space</h1> 

**Goal:**  
Practice translating an existing sound composition into a **spatial installation environment** using the lights, speakers, and objects available in the Lyons Family Studio (LFS).

You will re-design your previous work to function as a **multi-channel installation** in the actual space.

---

### Group Setup
- Form groups of **4–5 students**.  
- Each group will **choose one previously submitted work created by a member of your group**, from either:
  - **Week 7**
  - **Week 8**

---

### Timing (50 minutes)

- **10 minutes:** Group work (review the work + redesign spatial cues + prepare notes)  
- **20 minutes:** Selected groups work with the technicians to implement their cues in the LFS  
- **20 minutes:** Live demonstrations of the redesigned versions + brief class reflection

> **Note:** Even if your group is not selected to work directly with the technician, you are still expected to complete the full spatial redesign during class time.

---

<h2 style="color: darkred;">1. Select a Previous Work</h2>

As a group, choose **one sound composition** from a previous student submission (Week 7 or Week 8).

First, **watch the original submission together**.

Your task is to **reimagine how this sound could function** using the real lights, speakers, and objects in the LFS.

Think about:

- Which lights should activate
- How the space changes over time
- How the audience might experience the environment

---

<h2 style="color: darkred;">2. Redesign the Spatial Cues</h2>

Using the **existing objects and lighting setup in the LFS**, design a short sequence of cues that reinterpret the sound piece as an installation.

---

### Cue Design Requirements

Design **3–4 cues maximum**.

Your cues should define:

- **Which lights are active**
- **How intensity or colour changes**
- **How sound and light interact over time**

Focus on how the environment changes **as a spatial experience**.  

For each cue, clearly define:  

- Which lights activate
- Intensity changes
- Colour changes
- Dominance between lights

Make notes so you can **communicate clearly to the technicians**.

---

### Prepare to Speak to the Technician

You will communicate your cues **one group at a time**.

You must have:

- The **audio file ready on a USB drive** (USB provided by the instructor). 
- **Written notes** describing your cues

When speaking:

- Describe **actions**, not emotions
- Be **clear and concise**
- Assume you have **limited time**

Example:

> “Start the audio in the speakers while slowly fading in the side lights. After 10 seconds, reduce the the front lights at low intensity.”

---

<h2 style="color: darkred;">3. Presentation Format</h2>

Selected groups will present their redesign.  
> Select **2 students** to speak on behalf of the group.  

The sequence will be:

1. **Introduce the main idea of your redesign**
2. **Watch the original student submission**
3. **Explain the new spatial approach**
4. **Technicians execute the cues in the LFS**

Prepare:

- **1 sentence:** What spatial atmosphere did you want to create?
- **2–3 lines:** What changes did you introduce in sound and light?
- **1 line:** How does the new version transform the original work in the physical space?
