# <span style="color: darkred;">Final Portfolio — Individual Work</span>

## Objective

For the final assignment, you will assemble a **portfolio of revised works created during the course**.

The purpose of the portfolio is to **showcase the technical and creative skills you developed throughout the semester**, including:

- spatial staging
- lighting and atmosphere
- sound composition
- moving image integration
- hybrid media environments

Rather than simply resubmitting previous assignments, you will **review the feedback you received and improve selected works before presenting them in a curated portfolio**.

---

## Required Works

Your portfolio must include **revised versions of the following six submissions**:

- **W3 — Continuous Shot**
- **W4 — Lighting Exercise**
- **W6 — Sound Composition Scene**
- **W8 — Environmental Scene**
- **W9–10 — Multi-Channel Installation Design**
- **W11 — Hybrid Media Scene**

These works represent the **core techniques explored in the course** and demonstrate your progression from early spatial exercises to more complex hybrid media environments.

Before including them in your portfolio, **review the feedback you received and improve the work where possible**.

Revisions may include:

- improving lighting balance
- correcting framing or camera placement
- adjusting sound levels or timing
- refining spatial composition
- correcting technical mistakes
- improving visual clarity

The goal is not perfection, but **evidence of thoughtful revision and improvement**.

---

## Portfolio Format

Submit your portfolio as **one single PDF document**.

Each project should appear in its **own clearly labeled section**.

For each project include:

### Project Title
Example: *W6 — Sound Composition Scene*

### Short Description (3–5 sentences)

Explain:

- the idea or approach of the work
- the relationship between sound, image, and space
- the main concept explored in the assignment

### Revision Notes (2–4 sentences)

Briefly explain **what you improved or changed after receiving feedback**.

Examples:

- lighting adjustments
- sound balancing
- camera framing corrections
- spatial improvements
- editing changes

### Documentation Image

Include **one still image exported from Blender** showing the scene clearly.

Use a **wide view** so the scene, lighting, and objects are visible.

### Video Link

Provide a link to the video documentation of the work.

Videos must be uploaded to:

- **YouTube**, or
- **Vimeo**

Your video may be:

- **Public**, or  
- **Unlisted**

Make sure the link **can be accessed without login permissions**.

---

## Optional: Documentation Images

You may include **additional images** that help explain how your scene was constructed.

These are optional but recommended if they clarify your process.

Examples include:

- scene setup images
- lighting setup views
- video projection surfaces
- Blender interface screenshots

Include **short captions** explaining what each image shows.

---

## Portfolio Organization

Arrange the projects in **chronological order** to show the progression of your work across the semester.

Example structure:

1. W3 — Continuous Shot  
2. W4 — Lighting Exercise  
3. W6 — Sound Composition Scene  
4. W8 — Environmental Scene  
5. W9–10 — Multi-Channel Installation Design  
6. W11 — Hybrid Media Scene

Your portfolio will be evaluated based on:

### Revision & Improvement

Evidence that feedback from earlier assignments was considered and applied.

### Clarity of Documentation

### Organization & Presentation
