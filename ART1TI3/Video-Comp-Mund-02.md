## Objective

## Objective

Create a **first iteration (rough cut)** of your experimental video composition using only the photographs from Part 1. This version should explore rhythm, repetition, movement, and transitions using non-linear editing in **DaVinci Resolve**.

The goal is to experiment with visual storytelling and dynamic editing techniques—not to create a polished final piece. This draft will serve as a foundation for future revisions.


---

## Software Required

- DaVinci Resolve (download [here](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve))  
- Headphones (recommended for audio design)  
- Your computer (editing is not recommended on phones/tablets)

---

## Activities  
**Complete the following in order. Ask your professor or TA for help as needed.**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[40 min] Learn the Tools</h3>

### Create 2 short test videos using DaVinci Resolve.

Follow the provided beginner tutorials and practice:

1. **Test Video 1: Intro to DaVinci Resolve**  
   - Learn timeline navigation, cuts, imports, transitions  
2. **Test Video 2: Collage Techniques**  
   - Experiment with position, opacity, filters, and sound

➡️ Export both as **MP4 files**

📄 **Filenames:**  
- `Lastname-Name-TestVideo1.mp4`  
- `Lastname-Name-TestVideo2.mp4`

> 📝 Submit both test videos before you begin your final composition.

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[20 min] Conceptualize Your Video</h3>

Meet with your group and open a document to plan your final piece.

### Conceptual
- What mood, story, or atmosphere do we want to convey?
- How do our selected photographs support this?

### Artistic
- What transitions, audio, or effects will we use to elevate the visuals?
- What experimental techniques might we include?

### Technical
- What aspect ratio fits our idea (e.g., horizontal, vertical)?
- How will we animate images using keyframes (e.g., scale, opacity, position)?

➡️ **Export as PDF**  
**Filename:** `Group-#-Notes.pdf`  
Include **all names + student numbers**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[40 min] Create Final Video</h3>

Edit your final video collaboratively in **DaVinci Resolve**.

### Guidelines:
- Use **10–15 photographs only**  
- Length: **1–2 minutes**  
- Include **movement, layering, and transitions**  
- Maintain a minimum resolution of **720p**  

### Allowed tools:
- Keyframe animation (zoom, pan, opacity)
- Transitions (crossfades, wipes, etc.)
- Filters, effects, and colour grading
- Royalty-free music or ambient audio
- Titles and text (as needed)

❌ **No external visual material allowed**  
✅ **Only use images from Part 1**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[20 min] Review + Export</h3>

### Self-Critique
- What works well in the video?
- Is the pacing coherent and engaging?
- Are there technical issues (e.g., audio levels, pixelation)?

### Final Touches
- Add a **title screen** at the beginning  
- Add **credits** with group member names at the end

➡️ Export the final video as an **MP4**

📄 **Filename:** `Group-#-FinalVideo.mp4`
