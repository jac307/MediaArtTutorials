## Objective

<h3 style="color: darkred;">Set Up Your Camera for Photos</h3>

Make sure your camera is set correctly:

- File format: **JPEG**
- Enable **gridlines** or **rule of thirds**  
- Clean your lens  
- Test focus and exposure
