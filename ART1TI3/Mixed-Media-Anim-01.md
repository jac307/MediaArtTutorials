## Objective

Create a **30-second storytelling short** using video footage and **analogue mixed-media techniques**. This composition will serve as the base for your final mixed-media animation, combining photography, video, and physical interventions such as painting, drawing, collage, or textures.

---

## References

- [How to TELL A STORY in 30 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQY-jZrN5aA)  
- [Transforming my Cinematography with Mixed Media Animation Vol. 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1tOaWcN-P88)

---

## Materials Required

- Smartphone or personal camera  
- Computer with any video editing software (e.g., CapCut, iMovie, DaVinci Resolve)  
- Optional: tripod or stabilizer

---

## Activities  
**Complete the following in order. Ask your professor or TA for support or feedback.**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[20 min] #1: Conceptualize Your Project</h3>

Work in groups. Open a Word or Google Doc and answer the following:

### Conceptual
- What is the core message or emotion you want to convey?
- How will you visually structure your story in 30 seconds? (Beginning → Middle → End)

### Artistic
- What framing techniques will you use to enhance the visual style?

### Technical
- Where will you film? (Indoors is recommended for consistent artificial lighting)

➡️ **Export your answers as a PDF**  
📄 **Filename:** `Group-#-Notes.pdf` (Replace # with your group number)

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[15 min] #2: Set Up Your Equipment</h3>

Prepare your device for cinematic video recording.

### Required Settings:
- Activate gridlines for composition  
- Use high-resolution video mode (1080p minimum)  
- Lock focus and exposure if possible  

### Tutorials:
- [General] How to Make ANY Video Cinematic  
- [iPhone] The BEST iPhone Video Settings for Quality Footage  
- [Samsung] Simple Video Settings for Galaxy Devices

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[40 min] #3: Capture Your Footage</h3>

🎥 **Task:** Film your raw footage for the 30-second composition.

### Recording Tips:
- Record more footage than needed to allow flexibility in editing  
- Maintain steady shots with good lighting  
- Apply cinematic principles from class:
  - **Composition Structures:** Rule of Thirds, Symmetry, Leading Lines, Angles  
  - **Camera Shots:** Closeup, Medium Shot, Full Shot, etc.

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[20–30 min] #4: Edit Your Video</h3>

🎬 **Task:** Edit a 30-second video using CapCut or any video editing tool.

### Editing Guidelines:
- Must be in **black and white**
- Avoid underexposure — adjust brightness and contrast as needed  
- Ensure **story clarity** and **smooth transitions**

➡️ **Export as MP4**  
📄 **Filename:** `Group-#-Video.mp4` (Replace # with your group number)

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[30 min] #5: Create Grids for Printing</h3>

🎞️ **Task:** Convert your video into printable frames for mixed-media animation.

### Instructions:
1. **Export video as images** using VLC Media Player:  
   Tutorial: *VLC – Export Video as Images*
2. **Divide frames** equally among all group members.
3. Each student creates a personal frame grid:
   - **Document Size:** Letter (8.5 × 11 in)
   - Orientation: Portrait or Landscape
   - Max 2 columns per page
   - Even spacing between frames
   - Use: PowerPoint, Canva, Keynote, Google Slides, etc.

➡️ **Export as PDF**  
📄 **Filename:** `Group-#-Photogrid-FirstLastName.pdf`

📌 **Before next class (Feb 12)**: Print your grids in black and white and bring them to class for *Part 2*.
