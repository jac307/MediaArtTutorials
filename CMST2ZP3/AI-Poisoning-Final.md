## Step 1: Final Fixes

- Review all **final images** as a group.  
- Ensure **visual and thematic cohesion** across the series.  
- Confirm:
  - Aspect ratios and resolutions are consistent  
  - Colours, glitch styles, and compositions align with the group’s aesthetic plan  
  - Nightshade-poisoned images and unpoisoned images both integrate meaningfully in the sequence  
  - Captions, titles, or text elements are accurate and complete  
- Make any last adjustments to layout, colour, or sequencing. 

➡️ Finalize your **curated arrangement** of 3/4 images per student.

## Step 2: Final Group Submission Document

Each group must submit a **PDF** file titled:

📄 **Filename:**  `GlitchSeries-Group-#.pdf`  

This document must include:

1. **Project Title and Short Description**  
   - A clear summary of your concept, glitch methodology, and critical/artistic focus.  

2. **Author Names**  
   - Full names of all group members.

3. **Final Image Series**  
   - All final curated images in your chosen sequence, grid, collage, or narrative layout.  
   - Maintain consistent formatting (aspect ratio, sizing, tone).
   - For each image, include:  
     - Copilot-generated image (with remix additions)  
     - Nightshade-poisoned version  
     - Pollo.ai result  
     - Short caption explaining the creative/critical intentions  

5. **Credits**  
   - AI Citation Reference for each prompt used on Microsoft Co-Pilot and Pollo.ai
     > Include **only prompts relevant to the final image series**.

### General AI Citation Reference  

“[Generative AI tool]. (YYYY/MM/DD of prompt). *Text of prompt*. Generated using [Name of Tool]. Website of tool.”  

**Example:**  
“ChatGPT4. (2023/05/31). *Suggest a cookie recipe that combines oatmeal, chocolate chips, eggs, and sugar.* Generated using OpenAI’s ChatGPT. https://chat.openai.com”
