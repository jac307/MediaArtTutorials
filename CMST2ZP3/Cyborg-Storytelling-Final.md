## Objective  
Complete your final Cyborg Storytelling AR Storybook using Pictarize Studio.  
Each group must test, refine, and finalize their interactive AR experience. Submit all final materials as outlined below.

---

## Final Checklist

All components must be complete, tested, and submitted by the deadline.

---

## Step 1: Test + Final Fixes

- Test your **full Pictarize AR project** using a phone or tablet.
- Ensure all analogue images (targets) are scanned or photographed clearly.
- Confirm that all **digital overlays** (text, audio, video, 3D objects) are working properly.
- Fix any issues with:
  - Loading times  
  - Audio or video playback  
  - Alignment between analogue image and digital content  
  - Broken or missing media
- Work as a group to ensure **a cohesive and complete user experience.**

➡️ Build Project and Save the Link / QR Code.  

---

## Step 2: Final Group Submission Document

Each group must submit a PDF file titled:

📄 **Filename:**  `ARStorybook-Group-#.pdf`  

This document must include:

1. **Project Title and Short Description**  
   - Clearly summarize your story, aesthetic tone, and central speculative world.

2. **Author Names**  
   - Full names of all group members.

3. **Live Project Link**  
   - A working link/QR-code to your published Pictarize project.

4. **Trigger Images (Targets)**  
   - All analogue images used as AR triggers.
   - Insert a clear image of each one (e.g., scanned drawing, photo of textile, printed texture).
   - Label them so they are easy to test.

5. **2–3 Screenshots of AR in Action**  
   For each, include:
   - The **analogue image** used  
   - The **digital materials** layered on top (e.g., text, audio, GIF, video)  
   - A short caption describing the **story, mood, or theme** of the interaction

6. **Credits**  
   - APA-style citations for all public/open-source media used (including images, sounds, and videos).  
   - Clearly distinguish what your group created vs what was sourced.

---

## Step 3: Individual Research Report

Each student must submit a **research report** as a separate PDF:  

📄 **Filename:**  `ResearchReport-Lastname.pdf`

Must include:

- Full Name and Student Number  
- Title of Assigned Theme (e.g., BIPOC Futurism)  
- 300–400 word research analysis  
   - Connect one academic and two public sources to the project  
   - Reflect on identity, speculative design, and cultural/technological ideas  
- Annotated Bibliography  
   - APA-style citation + 3–5 sentence summary for each of the 3 sources

---

## Step 4: Group Self-Assessment

📄 **Filename:** `SelfAssessment-Group-#.pdf`  

Download and complete the [Cyborg Storytelling using Augmented Reality Self Assessment Form](materials/Project-Opt1-Assessment.pdf){:target="_blank"}. 
Your responses must thoughtfully reflect on:

- Collaboration and participation

- Critical thinking and connection to theory  
- Technical implementation  
- Final outcomes and storytelling impact
