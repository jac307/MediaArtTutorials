<h1 style="color: darkred;">Desktop Cinema / SMS Storytelling</h1>  
<p><strong>Groups of 2–3 students · Session 2</strong></p>

## 🎯 Objective  
Students will bring their desktop cinema narrative to life by recording, assembling, editing, and post-producing their planned 1-minute digital story.

> **Note:** *“Desktop cinema”* refers to narratives that are exclusively visualized through screens, emulating the experience of watching a film or documentary on a desktop or mobile device—where digital interactions and messaging serve as the primary medium for storytelling.

---

## 🧰 Materials Required

- Personal devices (laptops, tablets, or smartphones for editing)  
- Gathered or created media assets (images, sounds, GIFs, screenshots, etc.)  
- <a href="https://m365.cloud.microsoft/launch/Clipchamp/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Clipchamp</a> (Video editing software — access with McMaster email)  
- Headphones (for reviewing audio elements)  
- Self-Assessment Form (available digitally on A2L)  

---

## 🧩 Activities  
**Complete the following in order. Ask your professor or TA for help or feedback as needed.**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[15 minutes] 1. Quick Review</h3>

- Open and review your **Project Planning Document** and **Storyboard**  
- Confirm all needed materials (images, GIFs, sounds) are ready  
- Make last-minute adjustments to the storyboard if needed  

📋 **Check-in**: The professor will verify that your group has brought the required multimedia materials.  
> This check-in counts toward your project grade.

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[45 minutes] 2. Production and Assembly</h3>

- Begin assembling your video using <a href="https://m365.cloud.microsoft/launch/Clipchamp/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Clipchamp</a>  
- Log in with your **McMaster email credentials**

**Tutorials:**  
- *How to create picture-in-picture videos*  
- *How to add a mobile frame to a video*  
- *How to add animated text to video*

**Assembly Guidelines:**  
- Simulate digital interactions (SMS, email, social media) using screenshots, screen recordings, or mockups  
- Arrange your sequence based on your storyboard  
- Add images, GIFs, sounds, or video clips to enhance storytelling  
- Maintain consistent pacing — final video length should be **around 1 minute**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[30 minutes] 3. Post-Production</h3>

- Refine **visual transitions** for smooth flow between messages or screens  
- Balance **audio levels** (if using sound or background effects)  
- Review your narrative for **clarity**, **pacing**, and **emotional impact**  
- Export your final video as an **MP4** file  

📄 **Filename:** `Video-Group#.mp4`

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">📝 Group Self-Assessment</h3>

- Complete the **Self-Assessment Form** thoughtfully  
- Reflect on your collaboration and creative decisions  
📄 **Filename:** `SelfAssessment-Group#.pdf`

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">📤 Final Group Submission</h3>

| Type        | File Name                  | Who Submits     |
|-------------|----------------------------|-----------------|
| Group Video | `Video-Group#.mp4`         | One per group   |
| Planning    | `Planning-Group#.pdf`      | One per group   |
