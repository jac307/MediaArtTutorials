<h1 style="color: darkred;">W10: Group Brainstorming, Project Planning, and Image Testing</h1>  
<p><strong>Research-Creation Project · Group Brainstorming, Project Planning, and Image Testing</strong></p>
<p><strong>Groups of 3–4 students</strong></p>

## Objective  
Each group will collaboratively define the **core concept** and **artistic direction** of your glitch art project. You will establish a unified theme, determine the visual style and tone, and develop a concrete production plan for generating, poisoning, and remixing a cohesive series of AI-generated images.

---

## Materials Required

- Individual **research findings** and **Process Journals** from [W9: Final Research Report + AI Data Poisoning Workflow](Cyborg-Storytelling-W9.md){:target="_blank"}     
- Laptop or tablet with internet access  
- [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/) – access with McMaster email credentials  

---

## Activities  
**Complete the following in order. Ask your professor or TA for help as needed.**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[30 minutes] Group Brainstorming</h3>

Begin by discussing insights from Week 9:  
- Key takeaways from academic/public sources  
- Ethical, technical, or aesthetic insights  
- Creative process reflections: prompts tested, unexpected results, tool surprises

Open a share document (Word or Google Docs - `Planning-Group-#`) and answer the following:  

### Brainstorm:
- What theme connects your research and creative tests?  
- How will your images reflect a **critical perspective** on AI, data manipulation, or glitch art?  
- What **visual/conceptual logic** will keep your images consistent?  

**Artistic Production Guidelines**  
- Your group must create a **cohesive series of images** reflecting a shared theme, aesthetic, and conceptual direction.  
- Each student is responsible for **4–5 images** that align with this shared logic.  
- Consider:  
  - **Presentation format:** sequence, grid, collage, or narrative progression  
  - **Medium:** illustrative series, photographic glitches, image–text pairings, etc.  
  - Your approach should **enhance the glitch aesthetics** and critical themes of the project  

### Include:
- A **working title** or central mood/theme  
- A **moodboard** to express your concept visually
  > A moodboard is a visual collage that communicates the overall style, tone, and aesthetic direction of your project.
  - Tools:
    - <a href="https://www.adobe.com/express/create/photo-collage" target="_blank">Adobe Express – Free collage generator</a>  
    - <a href="https://m365.cloud.microsoft/launch/PowerPoint" target="_blank">Microsoft PowerPoint</a> (use McMaster login)
- Bullet points explaining how each member’s research can shape their contribution

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[60 minutes] Define the Project</h3>

Complete your **Planning Document** by including:  
- **Tentative Title** – working title capturing core concept + tone  
- **Conceptual Focus / Theme** (3–4 sentences) answering:  
  - What is the central message of your series?  
  - What ethical, emotional, or political questions are explored?  
- **Artistic Focus** – define shared direction:  
  - What type of visual content will you produce?  
  - How will adversarial prompting + data poisoning be used?  
  - How do these connect to conceptual goals?  
- **Moodboard** – glitch style, colour palette, textures, tone  
- **AI Prompting Strategy** – describe shared logic for Microsoft Copilot prompts:  
  - Will you use consistent structures, keywords, paradoxes?  
  - How will cohesion be ensured across all images?  
- **Anticipated Risks/Challenges** – creative or technical obstacles 

➡️ Export your file as a **PDF**  
📄 **Filename:** `Planning-Group-#.pdf`  

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">Process Journal – Phase 2</h3>

**Individually**, follow the group’s **shared conceptual + aesthetic direction** and update your `ProcessJournal-Lastname.pdf` document with:

- Write/refine **8–10 prompts** based on your group’s theme/visual strategy  
   - Test prompts in Microsoft Copilot, keeping conceptual alignment  
   - Save all images in a consistent aspect ratio (1:1, 4:5, or 16:9) for presentation cohesion  
- **Remix or alter** your 8-10 images. Recommended tools:
  - [Photopea](https://www.photopea.com/){:target="_blank"}
  - [Adobe Express Collage Generator](https://www.adobe.com/express/create/photo-collage){:target="_blank"}  
- In your **Process Jorunal**, include:
  - All prompts used + corresponding images  
  - Brief notes (1–2 sentences) on each image’s success/relevance  

➡️ Export your file as a **PDF**  
📄 **Filename:** `ProcessJournal-Lastname.pdf`
