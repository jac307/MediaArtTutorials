<h1 style="color: darkred;">P2 - In-class Work 1</h1>  
<p><strong>P2: Research–Creation Project · Groups of 3–4 students</strong></p>

## Objective  
In this session, you will select an artist/collective from the course archive as the foundation for your **Research–Creation Project**. You will review their archival entry, identify key works, and brainstorm **2–3 creative response directions** inspired by their practice.

By the end of this session, you will have:  
- Chosen your artist/collective from the course archive.  
- Reviewed and discussed their archival entry in detail.  
- Identified **3–4 key artworks/projects** for inspiration.  
- Brainstormed **2–3 possible creative response directions** to explore further.  

---

## Materials Required
- Access to the course **Artist/Collective Archive**  
- Digital or physical notebook for idea capture  
- Laptop or device for group work  
- Access to an online document platform (Google Docs, Word Online, etc.)  

---

## Activities  
**Complete the following in order. Ask your professor for guidance if needed.**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[15 minutes] Artist/Collective Selection</h3>

1. **Review the Course Archive**  
   - Browse the available artist/collective entries.  
   - As a group, shortlist **2–3 possibilities** that align with your interests.  

2. **Make Your Selection**
   - The artist/collective should be different from Project 1.  
   - Each group must select an **unclaimed** artist/collective — confirm with the class list that no other group has chosen the same one.  

4. **Record Your Selection** in a shared document under a section called **"Artist/Collective Selection"**:  
   - Name of artist/collective  
   - Why your group chose them  
   - First impressions of their work  

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[20 minutes] Inspiration Review</h3>

1. **Read the Archival Entry** for your chosen artist/collective.  
   - Pay attention to biography, thematic focus, artistic methods, and previous works.  
   - **Record ideas** in your shared document under **"Inspiration Review"**
   - Discuss what stands out to your group.  
     - How does the artist’s background or lived experience shape their work?  
     - What recurring themes, symbols, or motifs appear across their projects?  
     - How do they use materials, media, or techniques to communicate meaning?  
     - What is something missing in the Archival Entry that you would like to know about the artist/collective or their work?

2. **Identify Key Works**  
   - Explore the resources listed in the Annotated Bibliography: follow the links, skim intros/abstracts, and note relevance, key ideas, and any gaps you want to investigate.
   - **Record ideas** in your shared document under **"Selected Artworks"**
   - Select **3–4 artworks/projects** from the artist/collective that inspire you.  
   - For each work, note:  
     - Title, year, medium  
     - Short (1–2 sentence) description  
     - Why it inspires you  
   - **Divide the works** so each student has one to research further.  

3. **Begin Follow-Up Resource Search (Individual)**  
   - For your assigned artwork, find **2–3 additional resources** that deepen your understanding of:  
     - How the work was created (techniques, materials)  
     - The concepts or ideas behind it  
     - Its connection to community, body, or place (identity focus)  
   - Record full references in **APA style** for later use.  

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[30 minutes] Concept Seeding</h3>

> This is not your final decision — it’s an initial “seed” for possible directions.  

**Brainstorm Creative Response Directions**  
   - Based on your discussion, outline **2–3 possible project ideas** your group could develop.
   - **Record ideas** in your shared document under **"Concept Seeding"**  
   - Consider:  
     - Themes from the artist’s work you want to explore  
     - Media, techniques, or processes you could adapt  
     - Potential messages or questions your piece could raise  

➡️ Save all of your **group work** as a **PDF**  
📄 **Filename:** `Group-#-P2notes.pdf`  
> Include all group members’ **full names** and **student numbers** at the top.  

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">Before the Next Class</h3>

- Bring your **notes on the additional resources** you found for your assigned artwork.  
- Each student must prepare an **individual PDF** with their new resources cited in **APA style**.
