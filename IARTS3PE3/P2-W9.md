<h1 style="color: darkred;">P2 - In-class Work 3</h1>  
<p><strong>P2: Research–Creation Project · Groups of 3–4 students</strong></p>

## Objective  
In this session, you will focus on producing **at least 50% of your final project**. This means working with your group to move from planning and drafting into substantial creation. You will ensure the work is divided equally, make significant progress on your materials, and refine your approach so the project remains cohesive and true to your concept.

By the end of this session, you will have:  
- Completed at least half of your artistic response project.  
- Ensured equitable distribution of work among group members.  
- Advanced your materials and creative assets toward final form.  
- Maintained clear thematic links to **identity** through **community**, **body**, and/or **place**.  

---

## Materials Required
- **Group Project Notes** and **Individual Notes** from [P2 - In-class Work 2](P2-W8.md)   
- All gathered materials, tools, and equipment needed for creation  
- Digital or physical workspace for hands-on making  
- Access to your shared document and file storage platform  

---

## Activities  
**Complete the following in order. Ask your professor for guidance if needed.**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[15 minutes] Progress Check-in</h3>

1. **Review Your Plan**  
   - Revisit your Project Definition and Drafting & Materials Gathering notes.  
   - Confirm everyone understands their responsibilities for today.  

2. **Adjust if Needed**  
   - Identify any missing elements or changes to the plan.  
   - Reassign or adapt tasks to ensure balanced workload.

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[65 minutes] Project Work Session</h3>

1. **Divide and Create**  
   - Each member works on their assigned tasks/materials.  
   - Maintain communication to ensure all parts remain cohesive.  

2. **Document Progress**  
   - Take photos, screenshots, or short video clips of the work in progress.  
   - Add them to your shared document under a section called **"Work-in-Progress Documentation"**.  

3. **Mid-Session Review** (Optional but recommended)  
   - Pause to share progress with your group.  
   - Give feedback and make quick adjustments if something is off-track.  

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">Before the Next Class</h3>

- Complete at least **50% of the project** before the next session.  
- Ensure all assets created today are saved, backed up, and accessible to the whole group.  
- Continue refining your assigned sections/materials individually.
