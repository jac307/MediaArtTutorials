## Objective  
In this session, you will bring your project to at least **80% completion**, ensuring all major components are in place. You will also begin compiling your **draft submission package**, which will be refined before the final submission.

By the end of this session, you will have:  
- Completed a substantial portion of your artistic response project.  
- Documented your progress.  
- Created a draft version of your submission with all required sections.

---

## Materials Required
- Project materials and works-in-progress from [P2 - In-class Work 3](P2-W9.md){:target="_blank"} 
- Access to any tools, equipment, or software needed for creation  
- Access to references, artworks, and APA citation resources  

---

## Activities  
**Complete the following in order. Ask your professor for guidance if needed.**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[60 minutes] Project Completion Push</h3>

1. **Aim for 80% Completion**  
   - Ensure all major components of your project are completed or nearly complete.  
   - Address any unresolved technical, artistic, or conceptual issues.  

2. **Work Allocation**  
   - Divide remaining tasks equally among group members.  
   - Keep track of contributions in your shared document.

#### Work-in-Progress Documentation

- Take photos, screenshots, or short video clips (**posted privately on YouTube**) showing your progress.  
- Add them to your shared document under **"Work-in-Progress Documentation"**.  
- For each item, include:  
  - **A one-line description** of what is shown.  
  - **The name(s)** of the member(s) responsible.  

➡️ Save your proof of work as a **PDF**  
📄 **Filename:** `Group-#-P2-WIP2.pdf`

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[20 minutes] Draft Submission Compilation</h3>

In a new document, include:  
- **Title** of the project  
- **Authors with Roles** (each group member’s name + role)  
- **Short Description** (3–4 sentences summarizing the project)  
- Sketches, work-in-progress images, photos, or screenshots of your project  
- **Inspiration Statement** (2–3 sentences about how the selected artist(s)/collective(s) inspired your work)  
- **List of Referenced Artworks** (image/screenshot, titles, years, and artists)  
- **References** in **APA style** (all sources reviewed from archival entry + new research)  

➡️ Save as a **PDF**  
📄 **Filename:** `Group-#-P2-DraftSubmission.pdf`  

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[20 minutes] Poster Presentation Brainstorm</h3>

As part of the upcoming poster-presentation session, you will **present your archival entry as a visually designed poster**.  

- **Goal:** Transform your PDF content into a **clear, engaging, and visually appealing format** suitable for public display.  
- **Format:** Tabloid size (11" × 17"), full colour.  
- In your group, create a quick **sketch or layout plan** for how your information will be arranged on the poster.  
- Consider:  
  - Which images will serve as focal points?  
  - How will you balance text and visuals for readability?  
  - What visual style, typography, or colour palette reflects your artist’s/collective’s work?  
  - How will you clearly convey thematic connections between the selected artworks?  

*Tip:* Think of your poster as a curated window into your research — it should inform, attract, and leave a memorable impression.  

➡️ Save your design plan as a **PDF**  
📄 **Filename:** `Group-#-P2-PosterPlan.pdf`
