<h1 style="color: darkred;">P1 – In-class Work 3</h1>  
<p><strong>P1: Embodied Archival Entry · Groups of 2 students</strong></p>

## Objective  
In this session, you will merge your individually written sections into a **single, unified draft** with a consistent tone and voice, collaboratively writing the opening and closing sections. You will also begin preparing the **supporting materials** for your archival entry.  

By the end of this session, you will have:  
- Reviewed and refined your individual analysis into a single, cohesive draft.  
- Written the **opening** and **closing** sections together.  
- Gathered all required **archival entry materials**.  
- Compiled a single PDF with your analysis, materials, and annotated bibliography.

---

## Materials Required
- Draft analysis from **P1 – In-class Work 2**  
- Laptop or device for collaborative editing  
- Shared document platform (Google Docs, Word Online, etc.)  
- Annotated bibliography file  
- Images, videos, or documentation of selected artworks

---

## Activities  
**Complete the following in order. Ask your professor for guidance if needed.**

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[30 minutes] Collective Review of Individual Analysis</h3>

1. **Share & Compare**  
   - Read each partner’s analysis aloud.  
   - Take notes on:  
     - Overlaps and differences in focus  
     - Inconsistencies in tone, perspective, or vocabulary  
     - Strong phrases or ideas to keep  

2. **Merge into a Single Draft**  
   - Combine both paragraphs into one continuous text in your shared document.  
   - Edit for:  
     - A **consistent voice** (avoid the “two author” feeling)  
     - Smooth transitions between paragraphs  
     - Cohesion in argument and style  

> ❗ *Reminder:* This must be a **unique, creative analysis** — avoid generic descriptions.

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[25 minutes] Draft Opening & Closing Sections</h3>

**Opening (40–60 words)**  
- Introduce the artist/collective and your analysis focus.  
- Mention the two selected artworks.  

**Closing (40–60 words)**  
- Synthesize both analyses by highlighting shared themes or ideas.  
- End with a statement about the relevance, impact, or significance of the work.  

➡️ Add these directly into your shared document so you now have a **complete first draft**.

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[20 minutes] Review Full Draft</h3>  
- Read the full draft aloud to check flow and coherence.  
- Adjust tone, sentence structure, and word choice to read as a **single, cohesive piece**.  
- Verify that APA citations are correct and consistent.

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">[25 minutes] Gather Archival Entry Materials</h3>

Create a section in your document called **"Archival Entry Materials"** including:  
- **Artist/Collective Bio (100 words)**  
- **Country** of origin  
- **Two–Three Images/Screenshots** of selected artworks/projects with:  
  - *Title of work*  
  - *Year*  
  - *Technical specifications* (medium, dimensions, duration, etc.)  
  - *1–2 sentence description*  
  - *Link* to the work/project if available online

---

<h3 style="color: darkred;">📤 Final PDF for This Stage</h3>

By the end of this session, **combine the following into one PDF**:  
1. Artist/Collective Bio + Country  
2. Images/screenshots with full details for each artwork  
3. 300–400 word full draft analysis (opening, body, closing)  
4. Annotated bibliography  

📄 **Filename:** `P1-DraftEntry-W4-Lastname1-Lastname2.pdf`
