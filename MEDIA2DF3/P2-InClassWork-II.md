## Goal

During Part 2, each student will complete their **first individual composition** and develop their **second individual composition**. The pair will also update their **Adobe InDesign planning document** and prepare a layout including four compositions and an original photograph ready for printing.

---

## Software

- **Adobe InDesign** — planning and project documentation
- **Adobe Illustrator** — vector-based composition and illustration
- **Adobe Photoshop** — raster-based composition, image manipulation, collage, texture, and digital painting

> **Do not use Image Trace in Adobe Illustrator or automated tracing techniques to generate the compositions.**

## Complete the activity in three stages

Complete the following stages in order.

Each student will create/complete their **second individual composition: the abstract interpretation** of the assigned animal.

The abstract composition should transform the animal through formal and conceptual experimentation rather than simply reproduce its appearance.

Consider:

- Shape simplification
- Fragmentation
- Positive and negative space
- Repetition
- Scale
- Cropping
- Gestalt principles
- Texture
- Colour
- Spatial relationships
- Visual rhythm
- Hierarchy


The animal does not need to remain immediately recognizable, but the composition should develop from its **distinctive visual, anatomical, behavioural, or textural characteristics** identified during your research.

> Avoid creating an arbitrary abstract composition. There should be a clear relationship between your research, concept, and final visual decisions.

#### Document setup

The second composition must use the **same dimensions, aspect ratio, and orientation** selected by the pair during Part 1.

Approved formats are:

| Format | Orientation |
|---|---|
| **6 × 8 in** | Portrait |
| **8 × 6 in** | Landscape |
| **6 × 6 in** | Square |
| **7 × 7 in** | Square |

> All four compositions and the original photograph must use the **same dimensions and aspect ratio**.

##### Adobe Illustrator

If working in Illustrator:

| Setting | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Units | Inches |
| Size | Same dimensions selected by the pair |
| Bleed | 0.3 in on all sides |
| Colour Mode | RGB |
| Raster Effects | High — 300 PPI |

**Required layers:**

- **Guides**
- **Composition**
- **Background**

> The Guides layer must include an inner margin guide **0.3 in from the edges**. For submission, export the Illustrator composition as a **PDF with Preserve Illustrator Editing Capabilities enabled** so the layers and vector elements remain editable.

##### Adobe Photoshop

If working in Photoshop:

| Setting | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Units | Inches |
| Size | Same dimensions selected by the pair |
| Resolution | 300 PPI |
| Colour Mode | RGB |
| Bit Depth | 16-bit recommended |

Maintain clearly organized and editable layers.

> Do not flatten the working Photoshop file. Save the final editable file as a **.TIFF** for submission.

#### File naming

Save the second composition as:

- Illustrator: `Lastname-Firstname-P2-2.pdf`
- Photoshop: `Lastname-Firstname-P2-2.tiff`

> The file type depends on the software used to create the final composition.

#### Prepare the print document

Create a new **44 inches long Illustrator document**; height depends on your compositions. You must include at least 2 inches on the top and 2 inches on the bottom.

Import the final versions of all five images in a row:

1. Student 1 – Realistic or Figurative Composition
2. Student 1 – Abstract Composition
3. Student 2 – Realistic or Figurative Composition
4. Student 2 – Abstract Composition
5. Original photograph

For the four student compositions:

- Import the final **Illustrator/PDF vector files** when the work was created in Illustrator.
- Import the final **TIFF files** when the work was created in Photoshop.
- Do not flatten or unnecessarily rasterize vector-based compositions before placing them into the final Illustrator document.
- **Embed documents/images**.

> Leave at least 4 inches in-between compositions + at least 2 inches on the edges. 

#### Original photograph

Select a **high-quality photograph** of the assigned animal with enough resolution for the final print size.

You may:

- Crop the photograph to match the selected composition format.
- Resize the photograph as needed.
- Adjust its placement within the frame.

> **Do not distort or stretch the photograph.** Always maintain its original proportions when resizing.

The final photograph must use the **same dimensions and aspect ratio** as the four digital compositions.

#### Export File

Save printing file as: 

`Group-#-PrintingFile.pdf`. 

#### Final checklist

- [ ] Each student completed and revised both individual compositions.
- [ ] All four compositions use the same dimensions, aspect ratio, and orientation.
- [ ] **All imported documents/images were embedded.**
- [ ] The sheet contains the four final compositions and one high-quality original photograph.
- [ ] The print sheet preserves consistent spacing, space for trimming, and the original proportions of every image.
- [ ] Illustrator or Photoshop source files remain layered and editable.
