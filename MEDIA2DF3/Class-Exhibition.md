# Class Exhibition

## Goal

Prepare, install, and present work from **Project 1** and **Project 2** as part of a shared class exhibition. You will also contribute to the exhibition setup or cleanup and engage with classmates and visitors.

## Exhibition overview

As a culminating activity for the course, students will **participate in a class exhibition** showcasing works from **Project 1**, **Project 2**, and a **selection of in-class exercises**.

The exhibition will take place during **Week 13**. Check **Avenue to Learn** for the exact date, time, accessible location information, and setup schedule.

The instructor will organize the overall class exhibition and the display of selected exercises. Students are responsible for preparing and presenting their own **Project 1 and Project 2 works** according to the requirements below.

---

## Assessment

This activity contributes to the final course grade and will assess:

- **Project 1 and 2 exhibition presence and setup**
- **Participation in general exhibition setup and cleanup duties**
- **Engagement and presentation to exhibition attendees**

> You are expected to participate in the complete exhibition process. If a disability, access need, illness, religious observance, caregiving responsibility, or other approved circumstance affects your participation, contact the instructor as early as possible to arrange an equivalent way to meet the learning outcomes.

## Accessibility and participation options

- Divide speaking, installation, and visitor-engagement responsibilities with your partner in advance.
- You may use prepared notes, written or digital supporting material, assistive technology, or another agreed communication method when discussing your work.
- If lifting, carrying, cutting, mounting, standing for long periods, speaking with visitors, or completing another task creates an access barrier, contact the instructor to arrange an equivalent role.
- Keep entrances, doors, signs, and accessible travel paths clear. Secure all work and materials so they do not create trip, sharp-edge, or falling-object hazards.
- Make labels and supporting text easy to read. Use clear language, readable type, strong contrast, and image descriptions or alt text when digital material is part of the display.

#### Project 2 – Morphologies

Bring the **complete five-image series and all required mounting materials** to the exhibition.

Your Project 2 display must follow the exhibition-format planning developed during the project.

The complete series includes:

1. Student 1 – Realistic or Figurative Composition

You are responsible for mounting and installing the five-image series.

Bring all materials required for your planned presentation, such as:

- Foam board
- Illustration board
- Cardstock
- Adhesives
- Clips or supports
- Cutting tools, if required
- Any additional materials included in your exhibition plan

Your mounting strategy should demonstrate:

- Careful craftsmanship
- Consistent or intentionally varied spacing
- Clear alignment and organization
- Visual cohesion across the five images
- A meaningful relationship to the assigned animal or project concept
- Attention to the overall exhibition presentation

> Creativity is encouraged, but the final display must remain **stable, intentional, clean, and visually cohesive**.

The class will be divided into **two teams**:

- **Setup Team**
- **Cleanup Team**

> Check **Avenue to Learn** for the sign-up sheet, exhibition schedule, and exact arrival or departure times.

#### Setup Team

Students assigned to setup must arrive **1 hour and 30 minutes before the exhibition begins**.

Remember that you are responsible for **setting up your own Project 1 work** while also contributing to the **general exhibition setup**. Plan your installation in advance and arrive with enough time to complete your own work **without limiting your participation in the setup team**.

Responsibilities may include:

- Arranging tables, furniture, and display areas
- Helping install Project 1 works
- Helping organize selected in-class exercises
- Preparing the exhibition space for visitors

> **Being part of the Setup Team means contributing to the overall exhibition, not only installing your own work.** Plan accordingly so you have enough time for both responsibilities.

#### Cleanup Team

Students assigned to cleanup must remain for **1 hour after the exhibition closes**.

Responsibilities may include:

- Taking down class-wide displays
- Moving furniture and display materials
- Helping classmates remove their work
- Cleaning and organizing the exhibition space
- Returning, storing, recycling, or responsibly disposing of materials

> Each student is required to participate in their **selected setup or cleanup shift** or an approved equivalent role. Missing the assigned work without an approved alternative will affect the exhibition grade.

--- 

#### Engagement during the exhibition

All students must remain for the **full duration of the Class Exhibition** and actively participate throughout the event.

You are expected to:

- Visit and engage with **other students' work**
- Be present near your own work and available to **share information about your projects with visitors**
- Answer questions about your concept, process, technical decisions, and final presentation
- Discuss your work with classmates, visitors, and invited professors
- Support your group partner during conversations about the projects
- Remain actively engaged throughout the exhibition rather than leaving your work unattended for extended periods

> **Professors and other guests will be invited to attend the exhibition.** Be prepared to introduce your work clearly and discuss the decisions behind Projects 1 and 2. You may use prepared notes or another agreed communication method.

#### Exhibition opening

The instructor will select **one or two students** to give a brief introduction at the beginning of the exhibition as part of the **official opening**.

The introduction will briefly welcome visitors and introduce the exhibition and the work produced by the class.

> Engagement is assessed **throughout the exhibition**, not only when speaking about your own project.

## What to bring

Before arriving, make sure you have:

- **Completed Project 1 sculpture**
- **Complete five-image Project 2 series**
- **All Project 2 mounting materials**
- Any additional materials required by your approved exhibition plan
- Any tools specifically needed for your installation
- Your group prepared to discuss and present the projects to visitors

> Organize all presentation and mounting materials **before arriving for exhibition setup**.

## Plan your installation time

All students are responsible for **setting up their own Project 1 and Project 2 work before the exhibition opens**.

Plan in advance how much time your installation will require. Check **Avenue to Learn** for your required arrival time. The setup team must arrive **1 hour and 30 minutes before the exhibition begins**; other students may also need to arrive early, depending on the complexity of their installation.

Consider the time needed to:

- Assemble or position Project 1
- Mount and arrange the five Project 2 images
- Measure spacing and alignment
- Attach mounting materials securely
- Make final adjustments
- Clean and prepare the display area before visitors arrive

> **Do not assume your installation can be completed immediately before the exhibition opens.** Allow enough time to complete the setup carefully and have both projects fully exhibition-ready before the scheduled opening.

- [ ] We prepared notes, labels, image descriptions, or other communication supports we need.
- [ ] We know our setup or cleanup responsibilities, or we have confirmed an approved equivalent role.
- [ ] We are ready to remove our work and leave the exhibition space clean and organized.
