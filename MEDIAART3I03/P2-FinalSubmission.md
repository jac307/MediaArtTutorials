<h1 style="color: darkred;">Final Submission</h1>  

This **Final Submission Package** documents your **live performance** in a format suitable for:
- academic assessment
- artist portfolios
- future applications or showcases

The goal is to transform the **concert documentation** into a clear, well-presented record of your work.

📌 **Important:**  
The instructor will provide the **concert documentation files** (video + audio) via **Avenue to Learn** after the Open Performance.  
You are responsible for editing, organizing, and presenting these materials.  

---

## Project Info Sheet (Single PDF)

- **Submission format:** PDF file
- **Naming protocol:** `LastName-FirstName-P2-InfoSheet.pdf`  

⚠️ Failure to follow naming and formatting guidelines may result in lost points.

This document must include:

- **Project title**
- **High-Quality Representative image/photo**
- **Year of production**
- **Group members** (with country of origin, role, and short 50-80 words bio)
- **Duration** of performance
- **Description** (150–200 words)
  - Focus on concept, structure, and live elements
- **Link to Full Performance**
  **read requirements below)  
- **List of materials and technologies used**
- **Performance context**:
  - Open Performance (public concert)
  - Location and date (as provided on Avenue)
- **3-5 concert photos**. These should show:
  - the performance in the concert space
  - performers in action
  - interaction between sound, visuals, and space

🚨 *Reminder:*  
Concert documentation will be shared by the instructor via **Avenue to Learn**.  
You are responsible for editing and assembling your final submission.

---

### Edited Performance Video (Required)

Create a **single edited video** that documents the live performance.

**Requirements:**
- Combine:
  - the **two documentation camera angles**
  - the **direct audio recording** provided by the instructor
- Edit for clarity, continuity, and pacing
- Include:
  - **Title at the beginning**
  - **Credits at the end** (all group members + roles)

> This video should represent the performance accurately — not as a highlight reel, but as a coherent documentation of the work.

Upload the edited performance video to a **cloud-based platform**, such as:
- YouTube (unlisted or public)
- Vimeo
- Google Drive or similar

**Include a clickable link** in the PDF.
